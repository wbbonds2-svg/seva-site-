Our Story

How a statewide community meeting in Renton became a movement for civic participation.

Seva National Alliance USA was founded following a statewide community meeting held in Renton, Washington, in December 2025. The meeting brought together prominent Sikh community leaders and members from across Washington State to discuss the changing political landscape in America and the importance of greater civic participation.

During the discussions, there was a shared understanding that successful integration into American society requires active engagement in civic life. Community members agreed that building relationships with elected officials, encouraging civic involvement, and developing future leaders would strengthen both the Sikh community and the broader communities we serve.

Following the meeting, volunteers traveled throughout Washington State to meet with community members, visit local organizations, and share this vision. The response was overwhelmingly positive, with many people expressing appreciation for the initiative and a strong desire to become involved.

After months of conversations, community meetings, and collaboration, leaders from across the state came together to establish Seva National Alliance USA — created to unite individuals through service, civic engagement, leadership development, and community partnerships.

"Seva" means selfless service — work done for the good of others, expecting nothing in return.

The idea behind our name

While founded by members of the Sikh community, our programs are open to people of all backgrounds, cultures, and faiths.
